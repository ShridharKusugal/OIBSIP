How It Works

Effortless and accurate conversions in three intuitive steps.

01

Enter a Temperature

Type any positive, negative, or decimal value. Tempora automatically validates that the input stays above absolute zero.

02

ABOUT THE TOOL

A Simple Tool for Everyday Conversions

Tempora is a lightweight temperature conversion tool designed for students, learners, and everyday use. It converts values between Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin using standard mathematical formulas directly in your browser.

⚡

Results are calculated locally. No external API or account is required.

Standard Conversion Formulas

Fahrenheit to Celsius
°C = (°F - 32) × 5/9
Celsius to Fahrenheit
°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
Kelvin to Celsius
°C = K - 273.15
Celsius to Kelvin
K = °C + 273.15
Fahrenheit to Kelvin
K = (°F - 32) × 5/9 + 273.15
Kelvin to Fahrenheit
°F = (K - 273.15) × 9/5 + 32

Theoretical Minimum Limits (Absolute Zero)

Kelvin: 0 K
Celsius: -273.15 °C
Fahrenheit: -459.67 °F
